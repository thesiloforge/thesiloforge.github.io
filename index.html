About

The Silo Forge develops local-first desktop software for technical environments. Applications are distributed as native binaries that run entirely on your local storage and memory. Your logs, source files, and binaries never leave your machine.

Standalone tools use a perpetual license model. Buy a license once and keep the binary working indefinitely, with no phone-home mechanisms or mandatory subscriptions.
