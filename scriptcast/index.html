Pipeline

From script to mastered deliverables.

Deterministic processing executed locally from start to finish. Audio files are validated, synthesized, aligned, normalized, and packaged without leaving the workstation.

1

Preflight Sanity & Dependency Verification

Scans the physical project hierarchy, validates referenced audio stems across localized category directories, checks ComfyUI API endpoint health, and verifies FFmpeg/FFprobe system binaries.

2

Local Workflow Synthesis & Granular Queue

Dispatches text, reference audio, and generation parameters to ComfyUI over HTTP and WebSocket. Supports modular TTS and audio engine templates (CosyVoice, Breeze, OmniVoice, VoxCPM, and Stable Audio) with individual item regeneration.

3

Stem Normalization & Persistent YAML Cache

Calibrates individual audio files to category reference levels (Dialogue: -20.0 LUFS, Music: -32.0 LUFS, Ambience: -34.0 LUFS, SFX: -6.0 dBFS/dBTP). Measurements are stored in lufs.yaml to skip re-measuring unchanged files.

4

Sample-Accurate Timeline & Frame-Evaluated Ducking

Calculates timeline coordinates from physical audio durations and punctuation-based prosody. Generates an FFmpeg volume expression (volume=...:eval=frame) anchored to dialogue boundaries with grouping gaps and edge crossfades to prevent pumping artifacts.

5

Two-Pass Mastering, Tagging & Video Delivery

Applies two-pass loudness normalization (loudnorm) and peak limiting. Injects tags and chapter timecodes into FLAC, MP3 (320 kbps), and M4B (AAC 256 kbps) containers via ATL. Extracts promotional MP3 samplers and renders 1080p MP4 video using NVENC or software encoding with modular filter chains.

Capabilities

Signal processing and mastering defined in script.

Audio properties are declared directly in text or edited through dedicated application panels. The pipeline builds and executes FFmpeg filter chains automatically.

Avalonia Desktop Studio & SoundFlow In-Process Playback

Cross-platform desktop interface with virtualized asset lists, search and ungenerated filters, and in-process audio preview via SoundFlow (MiniAudio device backend), avoiding external process spawning and buffer underruns.

Multi-Language Sidecar Architecture

Maintain localized productions in English, European Spanish, or Latin American Spanish. Structural cues and timeline boundaries remain centralized in the master script while transcripts and audio assets resolve via language sidecars (e.g. script.es-es.yaml).

Granular Asset Synthesis & Task Queue

Synthesize entire productions or enqueue individual dialogue takes, character voice profiles, sound effects, and ambient beds into an asynchronous task queue with real-time execution tracking.

Calibrated Production Profiles

Configurable mastering presets targeting industry standards: EBU-R128 (-23.0 LUFS, -1.0 dBTP), ATSC-A/85 (-24.0 LUFS, -2.0 dBTP), Podcast-AES (-16.0 LUFS, -1.0 dBTP), and Audible-ACX (-20.0 LUFS, -3.0 dBTP).

Spatial & Environmental Acoustic Filters

Calculates distance attenuation with air absorption modeling, occlusion filtering, dynamic timeline pan sweeps (from start to end coordinates), stereo widening, and parametric acoustic presets (Room, Hall, Cave, Telephone).

Equal-Power Loop Tiling

Continuous ambient beds and musical drones are pre-processed into seamless loop tiles using a constant-power quarter-sine (qsin acrossfade) window to maintain constant signal energy across boundaries.

Video Packaging & Sampler Generation

Muxes mastered audio streams with visual cues into YouTube-compliant 1080p MP4 files using NVENC hardware encoding (fallback to libx264) with Ken Burns zoom-pan drift, grain, vignette, and opacity fades. Procedurally extracts and crossfades promotional audio samplers.
